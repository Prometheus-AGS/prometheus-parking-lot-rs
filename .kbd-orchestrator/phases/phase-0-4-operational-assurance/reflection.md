## Root Cause

The plan underestimated owner/environment work and cross-machine trust transport. Browser access, native OS control, provisioned toolchains and signed canonical state are different capabilities. Missing capabilities were repeatedly mistaken for product incompleteness until actual checks separated prerequisites, execution and authority. Receipts also crossed Windows/Linux filesystem semantics; Git-index source identity had to remain stable while real command paths stayed native.

The predecessor reflection recalled missing original signing authority, repeated after-hooks, incomplete raw gate logs and timing failures under load. Exposed authority and incomplete containment recurred as unresolved operational risk. This phase applied the task-boundary lesson: end-task consumed final task/change hooks once; archives were serialized through the managed runner; historical receipts remained historical. Actual current raw artifacts, same-run provenance and isolated quiet execution replaced inherited summary-only evidence.

The plan's recalled18changes/36tasks and unverified-environment notes in prior-context.md remain dated historical inputs. Actual two maintenance additions account for20/40; current production observations establish availability. They do not retroactively change the earlier knowledge state.

## Lessons Learned

- A working browser session does not imply native desktop control; establish and test Apple Accessibility/CoreGraphics capabilities through the actual Codex host before adopting a GUI observation requirement.
- Preserve failed and superseded evidence byte-for-byte; fresh passing observations and signed amendments supersede their applicability without rewriting history.
- A fresh CARGO_HOME does not stop ancestor Cargo configuration discovery or place rustc on PATH; isolate project location, Cargo home and targets and select the pinned compiler explicitly.
- Bind public evidence to immutable archive hashes and connected run/environment metadata; a transport receipt supplies no certification authority.
- Report measured performance regressions and dependency audit failures even when the adopted release decision accepts bounded scope and known costs.
